Nightstand Devices

Plug in a Kindle or Kobo and it appears in the sidebar. Its page shows what's on it, sends books in a format it can read, and brings back what you did on it: highlights become underlines in Notes, and a Kobo's reading position moves books to Reading or Finished. It all comes in as soon as the device is connected, and a summary shows what changed so anything can be taken back out.

Data
Library and covers from Open Library. Passages are real lines from the books

How it behaves

  1. Send the best file you have. If the library already has a format the device reads, it goes as is. Otherwise the EPUB is converted (AZW3 for Kindle), and the converted copy is kept in the library so it doesn't happen twice. PDFs are only sent when there's nothing else.
  2. Highlights come back as underlines. Kindle's My Clippings.txt and Kobo's database are read on connect. Each passage is matched to the book and its place in the text, labelled Quote, and added straight away. Anything already in Notes is skipped, and passages from books that aren't in the library are left out unless you tick them.
  3. Progress only moves forward. A Kobo position further on than Nightstand's moves the book along (Not started → Reading → Finished). A position behind Nightstand's is ignored. Kindle doesn't share positions for books sent over USB, so it never changes status.
  4. Removing from a device never touches the library. Books bought from Amazon or Kobo are protected, so they're listed but can't be copied in.

Decided

  1. No automatic sending. Books only go to a device when you send them.
  2. Highlights and progress come in automatically on connect. The banner and summary show what came in, and "Ask first" or "Never" are settings for anyone who wants them.
  3. USB only in the first version. No Send to Kindle email or Kobo Dropbox sync.
