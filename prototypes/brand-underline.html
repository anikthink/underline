Underline Brand

One idea: the line you draw under a sentence worth keeping. It sits in the mark, the wordmark and the reading screen, in the same ochre that marks a Quote. Everything else stays quiet so the books can be loud.

Open questions

  1. Which mark. I recommend A, the Underlined U. It's already in every prototype and it still reads at 16px. B is more literary but its serif blurs at small sizes. C says "notes app" more than "books".
  2. Tagline. None, or one of: "For the lines worth keeping." / "Read. Underline. Remember." It would only appear on the website and the installer.
  3. Icon finish. Flat, as shown, or a faint paper grain on the light icon? Grain looks richer in the Dock, but disappears at small sizes and in the tinted icon.
