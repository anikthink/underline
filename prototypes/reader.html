Nightstand Reader

What opens when you press ↵ on a book in the library. The text comes first, and the controls stay quiet until you need them. Underlining is the main action: select a passage and choose Quote, Idea, Question or Disagree. Notes sit in the margin, next to the line they're about.

01Reading

Paper theme, pages layout (the default). Underlines from earlier sessions, with notes in the right margin when there's room.

02Underlining a passage

Sepia. Selecting text brings up the four labels (Quote, Idea, Question, Disagree, or keys 1–4), then Note, Copy and Look up.

03Contents and text settings

Night. Chapters grouped by Middlemarch's eight books, each showing its underline count. The Aa menu sets theme, font, size, spacing, width and layout. With the contents open there's no room for a margin, so a dot marks each note.

04Notes

Every underline and bookmark in the book, grouped by chapter. Filter by label (here, Idea), by notes only, or by this chapter.

Decided

  1. Pages is the default layout. One page fits a 1280px window. A two-page spread appears when the window is wide enough. Scroll is still in the Aa menu, and the reader remembers your choice for each book.
  2. Underlines are labelled: ochre Quote, green Idea, indigo Question, rose Disagree. The labels appear in the margin and the Notes panel, and they filter the panel.
  3. Opening a book moves it to Reading. This applies to Not started books only; a Finished book keeps its status when you open it again. In the library prototype, "Start reading" in the book panel now does this and logs it in the activity history.
