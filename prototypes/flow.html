Nightstand The whole app, clickable

One window with every screen joined up: the library, the Reader, Notes, the Kindle and Kobo pages, ⌘K and Settings. They share one set of books and underlines, so what you do in one shows up in the others. Try the path below; each step ticks itself off when you do it. Keyboard works everywhere.

  1. 1Open Middlemarch ↵
  2. 2Select a line and underline it 1–4
  3. 3Find it in Notes G U
  4. 4Send a book to a device X S
  5. 5See it on the Kindle
  6. 6Do something from ⌘K ⌘K
  7. 7Change a setting ⌘,

What's joined up

  1. Opening a book moves it to Reading. Open a Not started book from the library or ⌘K and its status and the sidebar counts change.
  2. Underlines go straight to Notes. Select text in Middlemarch Chapter 35 and pick a label (or press 1–4). It appears in Notes marked Just now. Clicking it there opens the book at that line, and clicking an underline in the book lets you relabel or remove it.
  3. Sending updates the device. Books sent with X then S show a progress line in the sidebar, then appear on the Kindle's page and get the device mark in the library. Sending to the Kobo queues them.
  4. Settings change the app live. Theme recolours everything. Reader font, size, line length and theme change the open book. The device bring-in setting is the same one shown on each device's page. Export stays off until a folder is chosen.
  5. Not in this prototype: adding books and editing details, which link to their own prototypes; pages layout in the Reader (it scrolls here); the text of books other than Middlemarch.
