Nightstand Editing details

Details open in a panel beside the library, so you can fix one book after another without losing your place. Each field shows where its value came from. Anything you change is yours, and refreshing from Open Library never overwrites it without asking.

How it behaves

  1. One book saves as you go; ⌘Z undoes. Bulk edits wait for Apply, because they touch many books at once.
  2. Fields you change are yours. A refresh from Open Library shows them, unticked, and never replaces them silently.
  3. Series in the title. When Open Library's title contains the series ("The Dark Forest (The Three-Body Problem Series Book 2)"), Nightstand keeps the series out of the title.
  4. Shelves, rating and status are always yours and are never refreshed.
